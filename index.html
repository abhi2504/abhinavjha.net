I build software with a bias for clarity, speed, and craft.

I'm a developer who enjoys the small details — the difference between something that works and something that feels right. I spend most of my time building for the web, and I care a lot about how a product feels under your fingers: quick, honest, and quiet.

My approach is simple. Understand the problem first. Ship the smallest thing that solves it. Iterate from real use, not assumptions. I'm comfortable across the stack and gravitate toward systems that are easy to read, easy to change, and hard to break.

When I'm not building, I'm usually reading, exploring open-source, or quietly over-engineering my own tools.
